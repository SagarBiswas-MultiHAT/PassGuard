About this checker

How password cracking actually works, explained in 4 simple steps:

Step 1 Wordlist Check (The Quick Guess)

Attackers test billions of known breached passwords first (like 123456, password, admin). If yours is on that list, it is cracked in milliseconds.

Step 2 Smart Variations & Patterns

Attackers automatically try common twists like adding digits (password123), symbols (admin!), repeats (aaaa), or keyboard walks (qwerty). This checker detects these patterns and reduces your score.

Step 3 Combination Math (Brute-Force)

If your password avoids common words, attackers must guess every possibility. We calculate the total combinations based on length and character types (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols).

Step 4 Real-World Attack Scenarios

We divide the combinations by the speed of real attacker hardware across three realistic situations:

⚡ Offline Fast Hash (Worst Case)

A leaked database with weak encryption (MD5, SHA-256). An 8x NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU cluster tries 100 billion guesses per second.

🛡️ Offline Slow Hash (Well Protected)

A leaked database with strong security (bcrypt, Argon2). It forces attacker hardware to slow down to about 10,000 guesses per second.

🌐 Online Web Login (Live Defense)

An attacker guessing on a website login form. Defenses like rate limits, CAPTCHAs, and lockouts slow them to around 100 tries per hour.

ℹ️ Advisory: Real-world cracking speeds depend on attacker hardware and hashing algorithms. These estimates are benchmark calculations for educational guidance.
